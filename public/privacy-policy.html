Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Saut Ul Quran صوت القرآن اکیڈمی ("we", "us", or "our") handles user privacy for our mobile application and related web services.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in user privacy and Islamic values. Our application is designed to be fully functional without collecting any personal data:

  • We do not collect personal identification information (such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address).
  • We do not access your device contacts, microphone, camera, or gallery.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location data.

2. Local Storage and App Preferences

To provide a smooth Quran reading and recitation experience, the app stores minimal preference data locally on your device using local storage:

  • Your last read Surah and Ayah position (bookmarks).
  • Selected Reciter (Qari) preference.
  • Font size, Urdu translation toggle, and Tajweed color display preferences.

This information remains completely on your device and is never transmitted to or stored on any external servers.

3. Third-Party Services and Audio Streaming

The app plays Quran recitations from reputable Islamic audio content delivery networks (including EveryAyah and Archive.org). When audio is played, your device connects directly to these servers to stream the audio files.

If advertisements are displayed in the future (via Google AdMob), standard non-personally identifiable advertising identifiers may be processed in accordance with Google Play Services policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for users of all ages, including children, families, and students learning Quran recitation and Tajweed. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Security

We value your trust. Since we do not collect or store personal data on remote servers, your personal information is not susceptible to server-side breaches.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
